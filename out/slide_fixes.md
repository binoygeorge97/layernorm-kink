## Slide 17 (dpcwhy)

7. Formula line on the slide
   OLD: `‖∇L‖ ∼ e^(Nλ),   λ = closed-loop Lyapunov exponent of the rollout through the surrogate`
   NEW: `‖∇L‖ ~ e^(Nλ) (order of magnitude),   λ = closed-loop Lyapunov exponent through the surrogate`

8. Speaker notes, first paragraph
   OLD: `The DPC gradient is a sum over starts of products of Jacobians along each rollout: d x_k / d theta = sum over j of [product of (J_x + J_u K_i)] J_u d u_j / d theta. Its size grows like exp(N lambda), with lambda = (1/N) sum of log |J_x + J_u K_k| along the rollout, the closed-loop Lyapunov exponent through the surrogate.`
   NEW: `The DPC gradient is a sum over starts of products of Jacobians along each rollout. With the policy written as u = -K x (K_k = -du_k/dx_k), the closed-loop multiplier is m_k = J_x_hat - J_u_hat K_k, and d x_k / d theta = sum over j of [product of m_i for i between j and k] J_u_hat d u_j / d theta. Its size grows roughly like exp(N lambda), with lambda = (1/N) sum of log |m_k| along the rollout, the closed-loop Lyapunov exponent through the surrogate. The multiplier leaves the safe band |m| < 1 at BOTH edges: an input slope that is wrong-signed or too small (m > 1, J_u_hat < (J_x_hat - 1) / K) or too large (m < -1, J_u_hat > (J_x_hat + 1) / K); at A and K* the band is 0.29 B < J_u_hat < 19.7 B. On seeds 2 and 8 most expanding steps come from input slopes far too LARGE, not from the wrong-sign arm.`

9. Speaker notes, second paragraph, last sentence
   OLD: `exp(200 lambda) reproduces the measured gradient norms: seed 2 e^83.6 = 2e36; seed 8 e^31 = 4e13.`
   NEW: `exp(200 lambda) is an order-of-magnitude rule: it matches seeds 2 and 8 (e^84 ~ 1e36 and e^31 ~ 1e13 against measured gradients of order 1e35 and 1e13) but fails on seed 1 (lambda = 0.012 gives e^2.4 ~ 11, the measured gradient is of order 1e3). Quote gradient norms as orders of magnitude only: above about 1e10 they are not reproducible between computations (seed 9: 1.2e14 in one, 1.9e11 in the Task 0 re-run of 3 Nov; seed 2: 1.1e35 and 3.9e35).`

10. Speaker notes, last paragraph, last sentence
   OLD: `Low authority (B = 0.01, K about 10) amplifies J_u errors inside J_x + J_u K.`
   NEW: `Low authority (B = 0.01, K about 10) amplifies J_u errors inside m = J_x_hat - J_u_hat K.`
