## 1. The research in five sentences

A pre-norm LayerNorm residual block used as a learned dynamics model ("surrogate") of the scalar plant x+ = 1.03 x + 0.01 u
has a structural "kink": near one point z* = (x*, u*), fixed by the weights in closed form (Theorem 1), its input slope dF/du is
wrong by 7 to 200 times the true B, while its predictions stay accurate. Controllers that read the surrogate's slopes are hurt:
fixed LQR fails on 26.5 % of setpoint/start runs (static-norm surrogate: 0.6 %), rescheduled LQR fails where the path crosses
wrong-sign regions, and DPC (a GRU policy trained by backpropagating through the surrogate) is trapped on the narrowest kinks
(12 of 30 LN MSE 3000 runs) by an exploding, direction-chaotic gradient at the good controller. Two-stage training (MSE, then
1000 steps with a slope loss on labels estimated from the same data) removes every DPC trap and keeps LQR within the kink-free
range. The statistic Lambda* explains the GRU traps retrospectively but failed three prospective tests, so it is now stated as
an upper bound on the gradient, not a predictor.

## 4. Work in progress at handover

### 4.1 MLP mechanism (docs/RESULTS_mlp_mechanism.md, draft; post hoc; no claim changed)
Established: the MLP trap is NOT the GRU mechanism. At the MLP oracle the gradient is tame and stable in direction; on most
trapped seeds there is no barrier between the trapped controller and the oracle and the trapped controller is not a stationary
point; trapped policies park at a spurious equilibrium |x| ~ 0.2-2.5 that the TRUE plant shares (30 of 39), chatter in a
period-2 cycle near x = 0.1-0.35 (7), or diverge (2); saturation is NOT the cause (11-12 % saturated steps vs 8 % at the oracle;
99 % of the gradient from unsaturated steps); warm start at lr 1e-3 is expelled on 22/40 trapped vs 1/70 healthy runs, at
lr 1e-4 it stays. 11 of the 39 trapped runs were still descending clearly when training stopped (section 3a).
DONE (5 Nov), section 3b: +3000 epochs at N = 200 from the Run 5a controllers, fresh AdamW, lr 1e-3 and 1e-4, LN MSE 3000
seeds 1, 2, 8, 9 (trapped), seed 4 and two-stage seed 9 (healthy). No trapped seed reaches the oracle (within 5 %): after a
one-time drop when the optimiser state is reset (4.4-4.5 x -> 1.2-1.7 x the oracle's surrogate cost within 50 epochs at lr 1e-3),
three of four are flat at 1.2-1.7 x; seed 2 at lr 1e-4 is still falling slowly (1.5 x at epoch 3000). Healthy runs do not move.
Reading: the MLP trap is a plateau, not slow convergence; but part of its depth in Run 5a was an optimiser-state stall
(consistent with the Adam second-moment hypothesis, not tested directly). The owner decides whether the MLP trap definition
needs a converged-controller check (optimiser restart + plateau test) before MLP trap counts are quoted. Re-run commands:

## 8. Known pitfalls (in addition to CLAUDE.md section 5)

- Gradient norms above ~1e10 are not reproducible beyond order of magnitude: never compare them exactly.
- `pkill -f <pattern>` can match the shell wrapper rather than a `python -` child: check `ps` afterwards.
- A script without an `if __name__ == "__main__":` guard runs in full when imported (this happened once with
  theory/mlp_sat_park.py; fixed).
- Long CPU jobs die with the container: commit partial outputs regularly and note how to re-run.
- `out/predictor/warm_*.csv` (R5) cover 390 of 720 runs; the predictor search is stopped, do not extend it.
